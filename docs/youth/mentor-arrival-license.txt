Mentor digital arrival uses a GLSL adaptation of the Bayer threshold
approximation in three.js r185 examples/jsm/tsl/math/Bayer.js.
Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r185/examples/jsm/tsl/math/Bayer.js
The reveal timing, square-cell size, scatter, height bias and mint edge are
local adaptations; this is not an imported complete transition package.
