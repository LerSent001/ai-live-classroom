# Q-version TV waiting illustration

`vroid-chibi-turnaround.png` is an unchanged copy of the previously generated
`output/character-design/vroid-female-cel-turnaround-v1.png` (Codex imagegen,
based on the CC0 VRoid HairSample_Female reference). The full front/side/back
sheet is retained. The waiting-screen viewport shows its front third only.

The visual uses CSS floating, a soft shadow, sparkles and dots. It is not a newly
generated video or a rigged 2D animation. It does not call a generation provider,
show estimated progress, or affect lesson submission. Hidden pages pause it;
reduced-motion preference disables its movement. The existing first-frame gate
removes the illustration once real lesson playback starts.
